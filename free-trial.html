Is the trial really free?

Yes. Adults and teens can try one free kickboxing class and one free jiu-jitsu/grappling class. Kids get one free trial class. No card is needed to book.

Do I need experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, and many of our members started with no experience.

What should I bring?

Comfortable athletic clothes and a water bottle. We teach NoGi, so you don't need a gi.

How much is a membership after the trial?

Adult memberships start at $149/month plus tax with a 12-month commitment, and there's no registration fee. Kids are $130/month plus tax with a 3+ month commitment. See all pricing.

Where are you?

44 Prince Andrew Place, in the Don Mills area of North York. Please use the back door entrance.
